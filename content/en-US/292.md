The 'scroll-padding-inline-start' property defines the padding of the start edge of a scroll container when scrolling to an element. This property is particularly useful for controlling the alignment of scrolled content in horizontal layouts, providing a more visually appealing and user-friendly experience when navigating through items in a scrollable area.
