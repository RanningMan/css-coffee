The 'right' CSS property specifies the position of an element's right edge in relation to its containing block. It is commonly used in conjunction with positioning properties like 'absolute' or 'relative' to control the horizontal placement of an element. By setting the 'right' property, you can effectively move an element towards the left or right side of its container.
