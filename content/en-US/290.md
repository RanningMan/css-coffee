The 'scroll-padding-inline' property in CSS defines the padding area on the inline axis (left and right) when scrolling to an element. This property is particularly useful in creating a comfortable visual experience by ensuring that the target element is not flush against the edges of the viewport when scrolled into view, especially in horizontal scrolling contexts.
