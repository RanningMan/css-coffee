The 'overflow-inline' CSS property controls how content that overflows the inline axis of a container is handled. It can take values such as 'visible', 'hidden', 'scroll', and 'auto', allowing developers to manage overflow behavior effectively for inline elements. This property is particularly useful in responsive designs where content may not fit within its parent container.
