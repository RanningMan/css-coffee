The 'scroll-margin-inline-start' property sets the margin that is applied to the start of a scrollable area when the element is scrolled into view. This property is particularly useful for creating space around an element when it is focused or scrolled into the viewport, improving the visual layout and accessibility of content in a horizontal or inline context.
