The 'scroll-padding-block-start' CSS property defines the padding area on the block start side of a scroll container. This padding area is used to adjust the scroll position when navigating to an anchor link within the container, ensuring that the target element is not obscured by fixed headers or other elements. It enhances user experience by providing a more visually appealing scroll effect.
