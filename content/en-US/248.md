The 'overflow-x' CSS property controls the horizontal overflow of an element's content. It determines how content that exceeds the width of its container is handled, allowing you to set it to 'visible', 'hidden', 'scroll', or 'auto'. This property is particularly useful for managing layout issues when content does not fit within its designated space.
