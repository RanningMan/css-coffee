The 'place-content' CSS property is a shorthand for setting both 'align-content' and 'justify-content' in a grid or flex container. It helps control the alignment of the content within the container, allowing for efficient spacing and positioning of items along both axes. This property is particularly useful in responsive designs where content needs to be centered or spaced evenly.
