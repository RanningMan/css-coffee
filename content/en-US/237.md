The CSS 'orphans' property controls the minimum number of lines that must be shown at the bottom of a block container before it can be broken up. It helps prevent single lines from appearing at the top or bottom of a page or column, improving the overall readability of text. Setting 'orphans' to a value of 2 ensures that at least two lines of a paragraph are displayed together, avoiding the appearance of a lonely line at the end of a block.
