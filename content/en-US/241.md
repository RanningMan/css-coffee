The 'outline-style' property in CSS specifies the style of an outline that is drawn around an element. This property can take various values such as 'solid', 'dashed', 'dotted', etc., allowing for customization of the appearance of the outline. Unlike borders, outlines do not take up space and can be used to highlight elements without affecting their layout.
