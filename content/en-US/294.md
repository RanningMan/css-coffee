The 'scroll-snap-stop' property in CSS is used to control the behavior of scroll snapping when a user scrolls through a scrollable container. It determines whether the scroll position should stop at a snap point or not, allowing for smoother and more controlled scrolling experiences. This property can be particularly useful in creating carousel-like interfaces or galleries.
