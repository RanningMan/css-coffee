  overscroll-behavior-block: contain; background-color: #f0f0f0; padding: 10px;
  }

The 'overscroll-behavior-block' CSS property controls the behavior of scrolling when reaching the boundary of a scroll container in the block direction (vertical). By setting this property, you can prevent the scroll from propagating to the parent element, which can enhance user experience by keeping the focus within a specific content area.
