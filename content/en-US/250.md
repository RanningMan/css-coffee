The 'overscroll-behavior' CSS property controls the behavior of a container when it reaches the boundary of its scrollable area. It can prevent the default scroll chaining behavior, allowing for better control over scrolling in nested scrollable elements. This is particularly useful for improving user experience in mobile applications.
